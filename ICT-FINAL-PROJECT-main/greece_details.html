01 THE DESTINATION

A civilization of stories, islands and infinite blue.

Greece brings together some of the world's most remarkable ancient landmarks with dramatic coastlines, whitewashed villages and islands shaped by the light of the Mediterranean.

From the historic streets of Athens and the Acropolis to the cliffside villages of Santorini and the lively atmosphere of Mykonos, every destination reveals a different side of Greek life, culture and landscape.

This curated journey is designed for travelers who want to experience iconic history, island beauty, local culture and unforgettable Aegean scenery within one carefully planned Mediterranean escape.

07 / BEFORE YOU GO

Travel with the right information.

01

Best Time

Spring and early autumn are popular for comfortable weather and sightseeing, while summer offers the classic Greek island atmosphere with higher visitor demand.

02

What to Pack

Comfortable walking shoes, light clothing, evening wear, sunscreen, a hat, sunglasses, travel documents and a universal charger adapter are recommended.

03

Currency

Greece uses the Euro, which can be useful for local expenses, personal shopping and services not included in the confirmed package.

04

Visa & Documents

Carry your valid passport and required travel documents. Visa requirements should be confirmed before travel based on your nationality and current regulations.
