01 THE DESTINATION

Where the horizon feels closer than ever.

The Maldives is a world shaped by water—scattered islands, crystal-clear lagoons, soft white beaches and coral reefs surrounded by some of the most remarkable shades of blue in the Indian Ocean.

This curated six-day journey combines the energy of Malé, the relaxed charm of island life and unforgettable moments on and beneath the water. From peaceful mornings beside the lagoon to sunset cruises across the open ocean, every day moves at a slower and more beautiful pace.

Designed for couples, honeymooners, families and travelers seeking a refined tropical escape, the experience can be shaped around your preferred accommodation, activities and travel style.

07 / BEFORE YOU GO

Travel with the right information.

01

Best Time

November to April is generally popular for drier weather and clearer skies, while conditions can vary across the islands and throughout the year.

02

What to Pack

Light clothing, swimwear, sun protection, sunglasses, comfortable footwear and a waterproof pouch are useful for an island journey.

03

Visa & Documents

Passport, visa and entry requirements depend on nationality and current regulations. Confirm official requirements before travel.

04

Island Transfers

Depending on your accommodation, transfers may involve speedboats, domestic flights or seaplanes.
