01 — The Place

Kashmir unfolds as a journey of water, forest and altitude — with Arang Kel and Ratti Gali carrying the trip into its wildest frames.

The supplied programme is a nine-day Kashmir journey built around a sequence of mountain landscapes and outdoor experiences, with Arang Kel, Ratti Gali Lake, Taobat, Kutton, Sharda and Pir Chinasi among the named places.

Rather than presenting Kashmir as a single stop, the itinerary moves through contrasting settings: high-altitude lake scenery, mountain settlements, forested valleys and viewpoints. Camping, trekking, jeep rides and bonfires form the experiential layer of the programme.

The route is designed for travellers who want the landscape to remain central. The visual language of the trip shifts from open water and alpine terrain to village-scale encounters and slower evenings around camp.
