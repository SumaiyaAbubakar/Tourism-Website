01 — The Place

Skardu is less a single destination than a threshold into high country — where lakes, valleys and the vast Deosai plateau redraw the scale of the journey.

The supplied programme presents Skardu as a summer journey through mountain landscapes, with Shigar, Deosai and Kachura forming the core of its sense of place.

The route moves between the built character of Shigar, the high-altitude openness of Deosai and the water-and-mountain atmosphere of Kachura. Jeep travel and a bonfire add an experiential rhythm beyond sightseeing.

The result is a journey defined by contrast: dry mountain textures, expansive high-altitude plains, lakes, dramatic horizons and evenings around a fire. The supplied itinerary positions Skardu as a destination for both landscape and atmosphere.

The supplied checklist recommends a rucksack, warm clothing, rain protection, gloves, woollen socks, a cap, sunglasses, water, trekking footwear or durable joggers, a torch, power bank, charger, moisturiser, lip balm, insect repellent, tissues and basic first-aid medicines.

The supplied booking procedure requires Rs10,000 per head as advance. Cancellation deductions are stated as 50% at 7 days, 75% at 3 days, and 100% for less than 3 days before departure.
