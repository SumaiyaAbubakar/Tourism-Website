THE DESTINATION

Where the ocean becomes your escape.

The Maldives is known for its white-sand beaches, crystal-clear water and luxurious water villas. Its tropical island setting makes it one of the most popular destinations for honeymoons and unforgettable vacations.

From peaceful private islands to spectacular underwater experiences, the Maldives offers a unique combination of relaxation, luxury and extraordinary marine scenery.
